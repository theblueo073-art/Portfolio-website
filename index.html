About me

Curious about technology.
Committed to making it safer.

I'm Onuabuchi Fidelis Igwe, a Computer Science student, frontend developer, and aspiring cybersecurity professional. I'm passionate about building digital experiences, understanding how technology works, and helping make it more secure.

My interests bring together web development and cybersecurity. I enjoy creating responsive, user-focused websites while learning how to identify vulnerabilities, assess risk, and protect digital environments.

I work with frontend technologies including HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, turning ideas into clean, usable interfaces. Alongside development, I'm building practical skills in ethical hacking, penetration testing, network security, reconnaissance, and vulnerability assessment. My learning so far includes Linux and Kali Linux, as well as tools such as Nmap, Nikto, and Netdiscover, used in authorised environments.

I'm also growing my Python skills and developing ATLAS (Attack Surface Mapping), a personal project for discovering, mapping, and assessing the exposure of digital assets within authorised environments.

I believe technology should do more than work: we should understand how it works, consider where it can fail, and build with security in mind. My long-term goal is to become a well-rounded technology professional, contribute to meaningful digital solutions, and create innovative products and services through The Blue Ocean.

I'm always learning, experimenting, and building—and looking for new ways to grow.
